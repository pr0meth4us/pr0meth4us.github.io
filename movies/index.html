Movie Log

A personal collection of films I've watched and my thoughts on them.

Dune: Part Two

Denis Villeneuve 2024

An intense and masterfully crafted biographical thriller. The non-linear narrative and powerful performances make it an unforgettable experience.

Oppenheimer

Christopher Nolan 2023

A genre-bending masterpiece that is equal parts hilarious, thrilling, and tragic. The social commentary is razor-sharp and stays with you.

Parasite

Bong Joon Ho 2019

Visually stunning and philosophically profound. A worthy successor to the original that expands on its themes in meaningful ways.

Blade Runner 2049

Denis Villeneuve 2017

Wes Anderson's signature style at its most charming and whimsical. A visually delightful and surprisingly poignant story.

The Grand Budapest Hotel

Wes Anderson 2014

A mind-bending blockbuster that is both intelligent and immensely entertaining. The concept is original and executed flawlessly.

Inception

Christopher Nolan 2010

A magical and enchanting animated film that captures the imagination. A timeless story about courage and identity.

Spirited Away

Hayao Miyazaki 2001

Changed the landscape of independent cinema. The non-linear structure, sharp dialogue, and unforgettable characters make it a classic.

Pulp Fiction

Quentin Tarantino 1994
