Why Cambodia should legalize surrogacy

Published in S. Po (Ed.), Southeast Asia and Beyond: Analysis & Policy Pathways (pp. 64-73). Royal University of Phnom Penh.

The ethical, legal, and health concerns surrounding the ambiguous ban on surrogacy in Cambodia. This paper argues that Cambodia should revoke the ban and establish a structured, legal framework for surrogacy services to protect the rights of all involved parties.
